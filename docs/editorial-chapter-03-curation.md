# Editorial Chapter 03 – The City That Welcomes the World

## Purpose

This chapter introduces Turin as the city that welcomes Terra Madre Salone del Gusto.

It is not a travel guide.

It is not a list of landmarks.

It tells the story of a city where culture, public life and food naturally come together.

The visitor should feel that Turin is part of the Terra Madre experience rather than simply its location.

---

# Editorial Goal

Place before destination.

Atmosphere before sightseeing.

Experience before information.

The chapter should feel calm, authentic and welcoming.

---

---

# Photography Direction

Only four photographs are used.

Each image has a clear narrative purpose.

No decorative imagery.

No tourism clichés.

No image galleries.

No carousels.

---

# Layout Principles

Follow the Creative Bible.

Compact editorial spreads.

Approximately 700–800px height on desktop.

Photography and editorial statement remain within the same viewport.

Alternate image position naturally.

Maintain generous whitespace.
