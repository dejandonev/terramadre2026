# Editorial Chapter 04 – Macedonia in the Global Conversation

## Purpose

This chapter presents the role of the Macedonian delegation at Terra Madre Salone del Gusto.

It is not about exhibiting products.

It is about participating in a global community that shares knowledge, experience and responsibility for the future of food.

Each edition of Terra Madre brings together a delegation coordinated by Slow Food Macedonia, including members, producers, activists and partners who actively participate in panels, workshops, discussions and meetings with people from around the world.

The visitor should understand that Macedonia contributes to Terra Madre through dialogue, collaboration and shared learning.

---

# Editorial Goal

Participation before promotion.

Dialogue before presentation.

Knowledge before marketing.

People before institutions.

The chapter should communicate openness, curiosity and international cooperation.

---

> Every conversation at Terra Madre becomes an opportunity to strengthen sustainable food systems back home.

---

# Photography Direction

Prioritise photographs that show:

• participation

• dialogue

• workshops

• meetings

• exchange of ideas

• authentic interaction

Use product photography only when it supports the story of knowledge and cultural exchange.

Avoid suggesting that Macedonia has a national exhibition stand.

---

# Layout Principles

Continue the editorial language established in Chapters 02 and 03.

Compact editorial spreads.

Desktop height approximately 700–800px.

Photography and editorial statement remain together within the same viewport.

Alternate image position naturally.

Maintain generous whitespace.

No cards.

No galleries.

No sliders.

No decorative overlays.

# Success Criteria

The visitor should understand that the Macedonian delegation participates in Terra Madre to exchange knowledge, build international connections and bring new ideas back to Macedonia.

The chapter should inspire confidence that every journey to Terra Madre creates lasting value beyond the event itself.
