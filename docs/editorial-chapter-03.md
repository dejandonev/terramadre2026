# Editorial Chapter 03 – The City That Welcomes the World

## Purpose

This chapter introduces Turin as more than a destination.

It presents the city as the natural home of Terra Madre Salone del Gusto.

Visitors should feel that the city itself is part of the experience.

---

# Editorial Goal

Do not promote tourism.

Do not list attractions.

Tell the story of a city where culture, gastronomy, craftsmanship and public life naturally come together.

The destination should feel authentic rather than commercial.

---

# Closing Statement

> The destination is not only where we arrive, but where we begin to belong.

---

# Photography Direction

Prioritize authentic street photography.

Avoid postcard compositions.

Avoid excessive drone photography.

Prefer:

• streets

• arcades

• cafés

• markets

• public squares

• everyday life

• details

The city should feel lived in.

---

# Layout Principles

Follow the Creative Bible.

Compact editorial spreads.

Approximately 700–800px scene height on desktop.

Photography and editorial statement should appear together.

No full-screen cinematic sections.

No cards.

No sliders.

No galleries.
